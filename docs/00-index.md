# SafeArrival documentation

Read these documents in order. The first documents establish the problem and evidence; later documents should record decisions made after user research.

1. [Project brief](01-project-brief.md) — the initial problem framing and product concept.
2. [Desk research](02-desk-research.md) — published South African crime context, existing services, technical constraints, privacy considerations, and open questions.
3. [Interview guide](03-interview-guide.md) — a neutral plan for speaking with at least three people affected by the problem.
4. [Research synthesis](04-research-synthesis.md) — template for anonymised interview evidence and changes to the project.
5. [Product and technical design](05-product-and-technical-design.md) — research-led decision summary.
6. [Project setup](06-project-setup.md) — repository structure, stack, local commands, and current implementation status.
7. [System blueprint](07-system-blueprint.md) — consolidated requirements, behavior, ERD, system/sequence diagrams, API, security, hosting, and delivery plan. Open decisions remain provisional until research and deployment checks are complete.

## Evidence and privacy rules

- Keep desk research, interview findings, assumptions, and product decisions clearly distinguished.
- Use public statistics with their dates, definitions, and limitations. Do not turn national statistics into claims about a particular street or household.
- Do not write real names, phone numbers, exact addresses, vehicle registrations, or other identifiable incident details into the repository.
- Use made-up test data in the application, screenshots, and walkthrough.

The assignment brief requires a live hosted solution, full test access, code in a private repository with incremental commits, a short walkthrough, and evidence from at least three affected people for the own-idea route. Treat those as submission requirements, not optional extras.
