# Research plan

The internship assignment requires evidence from at least three people affected by the problem for the own-idea route. The project must be grounded in their needs, not only in the original idea.

## Before building the main flow

Speak with at least three people who regularly arrive home late or have responsibility for checking on someone who does. Ask open questions about their current routine and what would make a check-in useful. Do not collect identifying or sensitive personal details.

Suggested questions:

- Walk me through what you usually do when you are arriving home late.
- What parts of that routine feel most uncertain or difficult?
- How do you currently let someone know you arrived safely?
- What would make an alert useful, and what would make it stressful or unsafe?
- Would you use a journey timer, and what should happen if you miss a check-in?
- What should the app do when the phone is unavailable, offline, or its location is inaccurate?

## Record findings safely

Summarise anonymised themes, quotes only with consent, and product changes made from feedback. Do not record names, phone numbers, exact addresses, ID numbers, or other real personal data. Use fictional data for all product demos and test accounts.
